# Methodology Alignment

This site inherits the strategic intent of the parent repository's
`docs/methodology/` while remaining an independently deployed subdomain.

## AEO / GEO checklist

- [x] One intent and answer-first opening per locale.
- [x] One visible H1 per page.
- [x] Page-level title, description, canonical, hreflang, `x-default`, Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
- [x] Correct `<html lang>`: `en-CA` and `vi-CA`.
- [x] Organization/WebSite/Service entity graph with consistent `@id` values.
- [x] Visible FAQ and matching FAQ schema.
- [x] Programmatic bilingual sitemap.
- [x] Public content crawlable; API route excluded; no private route in sitemap.
- [x] `llms.txt` is short, factual and contains no unsupported performance claims.
- [x] Generated 1200×630 Open Graph image.
- [ ] Production source and schema validation after deployment.
- [ ] Search Console/Bing submission after domain verification.

## Claim and citation rules

- The 17% Canadian outsourcing statistic links to Statistics Canada and states its Q1 2025 scope.
- No “#1”, “only”, team-size, savings percentage, delivery count or guaranteed KPI appears.
- No HiTek project is represented as a Wizy project.
- No client logo, testimonial, award or certification is published without approval.
- Legal, insurance and compliance controls are described as engagement-specific rather than already certified.

## Sense-making rules

- The page helps buyers understand in-house hiring, fragmented freelancers and opaque offshore delivery without claiming Wizy always wins.
- A “probably not a fit” panel concedes three conditions where the current Wizy model should not be selected.
- Pricing structure is explained in the FAQ without manufacturing a public price.
- Buyer questions about team location, subcontracting, data access, IP, time-zone overlap and change control are answered directly and ungated.
- The qualification form appears only after the complete decision content; core information is not hidden behind lead capture.

## Intentional open decisions

- Public starting price remains absent until commercial inputs are approved.
- Named case studies remain absent until attribution and usage rights are approved.
- The official HiTek logo remains absent until co-brand permission is confirmed.
- Production validation remains pending until Vercel and Supabase are configured.
