===EN===
Some funding still exists, but less than in 2022–2023. The federal Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP) is closed to new applications. In Ontario, the Digital Modernization and Adoption Plan (DMAP), run through the Ontario Centre of Innovation, has offered matched funding to have an approved consultant write a digital adoption plan. The National Research Council's IRAP supports technology innovation projects, and SR&ED tax credits apply only to qualifying R&D performed in Canada. Most programs pay for planning or innovation, not for ordinary software purchases.

This article is general information as of November 2026, not funding or tax advice. Programs open, close and change terms often. Always confirm on the official program page before you plan around it.

## Key takeaways

- Do not approve a software budget that only works if a grant is approved.
- Planning grants and innovation programs are the most common options left.
- Eligibility usually requires an incorporated business with a Business Number.
- Apply before the work starts; most programs do not pay for work already done.

## What programs should Ontario businesses look at?

| Program | Who runs it | What it supports | Status to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Digital Modernization and Adoption Plan (DMAP) | [Ontario Centre of Innovation](https://www.oc-innovation.ca/) | A digital adoption plan written by an approved consultant. Reported as up to 50% of eligible costs, to a maximum of $15,000 | Intake windows and terms change. Check OCI's current page |
| Industrial Research Assistance Program (IRAP) | [National Research Council Canada](https://nrc.canada.ca/en/support-technology-innovation) | Advisory services and funding for technology innovation in incorporated SMEs | Contact an IRAP advisor; not a general digital adoption grant |
| SR&ED tax credits | [Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program.html) | Qualifying experimental development performed in Canada | Routine software development generally does not qualify. See [SR&ED and custom software](/en/blog/custom-software-sred-eligibility) |
| Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP) | Government of Canada | Previously funded digital plans and online adoption | Closed to new applications |
| Financing (not grants) | Lenders such as [BDC](https://www.bdc.ca/) | Loans for technology investments | Repayable; compare terms |

## Which program fits which situation?

| Your situation | Where to look first |
| --- | --- |
| "We know we need to modernize but not where to start" | A planning program such as DMAP, if open |
| "We are building something technically new, with real uncertainty" | IRAP advisor conversation; SR&ED review with an advisor |
| "We want to buy or set up standard software" | Usually your own budget or financing; grants rarely cover this |
| "We are a sole proprietor" | Many programs require incorporation; check eligibility first |

## How do you plan a budget around funding?

1. **Budget without the grant.** Decide the project is worth doing at full cost.
2. **Check eligibility early.** Read the official guidelines, especially eligible costs and timing.
3. **Do not start too soon.** Work done before approval often does not count.
4. **Keep records.** Invoices, timesheets and deliverables, organized by program rules.
5. **Treat any approval as a bonus** that reduces net cost or extends scope.

See [how much custom software costs for a Canadian small business](/en/blog/custom-software-cost-canada-small-business) and [how to plan a software modernization budget](/en/blog/software-modernization-budget-planning).

## Can a digital adoption plan become a software project?

Yes. A good plan maps your processes, data and tools, then recommends what to buy, connect or build. That is close to the first half of a discovery phase. If the plan recommends custom work, it becomes part of your brief. See [what a Product Discovery Sprint delivers](/en/blog/product-discovery-sprint-deliverables).

## How does this play out in a fictional case?

A 25-person food manufacturer in Mississauga wants to replace spreadsheets for production planning. The owner budgets the project assuming no grant. Separately, they check OCI's site for DMAP, confirm they are incorporated and within the size limit, and contact an approved consultant before any planning work starts. The resulting plan recommends connecting existing inventory software to a small custom planning tool, which becomes the brief for quotes.

## What should you watch out for?

- Consultants who promise approval or guarantee funding amounts
- Fees charged as a percentage of a grant you have not received
- Third-party websites with outdated program details

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a grant that pays for custom software development directly?

Rarely for routine business software. Innovation programs may support technically uncertain work. Check IRAP and SR&ED with an advisor.

### Can I use a Wizy plan for DMAP?

DMAP requires a consultant from its own approved roster. Check OCI's current rules for who qualifies.

### Do I need to be incorporated?

For many programs, yes. Sole proprietorships are often ineligible. Confirm on each program page.

### Where can I see all current programs?

The Government of Canada's [business benefits finder](https://innovation.ised-isde.canada.ca/s/?language=en_CA) and the Ontario government's business pages list current programs.

## Lập ngân sách quanh nguồn tài trợ thế nào?

1. **Lập ngân sách khi không có tài trợ.** Quyết định dự án đáng làm với toàn bộ chi phí.
2. **Kiểm tra điều kiện sớm.** Đọc hướng dẫn chính thức, đặc biệt là chi phí hợp lệ và thời điểm.
3. **Đừng bắt đầu quá sớm.** Việc làm trước khi được duyệt thường không được tính.
4. **Giữ hồ sơ.** Hoá đơn, bảng chấm công và sản phẩm bàn giao, sắp xếp theo quy định của chương trình.
5. **Coi khoản được duyệt là phần thưởng** giúp giảm chi phí ròng hoặc mở rộng phạm vi.

Được. Một kế hoạch tốt vẽ ra quy trình, dữ liệu và công cụ của bạn, rồi đề xuất nên mua, kết nối hay làm riêng cái gì. Điều đó gần với nửa đầu của một giai đoạn discovery. ## Một ca giả lập diễn ra thế nào?

Một cơ sở sản xuất thực phẩm 25 người ở Mississauga muốn thay các bảng tính lập kế hoạch sản xuất. Chủ doanh nghiệp lập ngân sách dự án với giả định không có tài trợ.
