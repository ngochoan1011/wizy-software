# Wizy Software Blog — Content Plan

> Last updated: 2026-11-16
> Posts live in `public.software_posts` and are managed at `wizy.ca/vi/admin?tab=software-posts`.

## House format (AEO)

- Title is the question a buyer would ask.
- First 1–3 sentences answer it directly.
- "Key takeaways" list, then H2s phrased as follow-up questions.
- At least one table or checklist, and one or two fictional examples.
- Internal links to related posts, a contact CTA, and 3–4 FAQs at the end.
- No Wizy prices, delivery promises, client names or compliance claims. Third-party figures are attributed with a link and labelled as not a Wizy price.
- Legal, privacy and tax topics quote the primary source (OPC, CRA, Ontario) and carry a "general information, not advice" line.
- Cover: 1280×720 photo of the `hoan` character (cremyX), no text in the image, stored in `blog-media/covers/software-<slug>.jpg`.
